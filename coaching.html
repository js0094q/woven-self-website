Turn Insight Into Action

You understand yourself better. Now what?

1:1 coaching provides focused, practical support for applying what you have learned about yourself to everyday decisions, relationships, and goals.

You've done some of the deeper work. Now comes the part where you figure out what to actually do with it. Together, we identify specific next steps and ways to follow through.

Explore coaching

What we can work on

The focus is practical and future-oriented. We start with a goal or decision that matters to you and work out how to move it forward.

Boundaries & relationships

  • Setting and maintaining boundaries
  • Practicing communication in relationships
  • Developing self-trust in everyday choices

Decisions & direction

  • Making decisions and planning next steps
  • Navigating life transitions and identity changes
  • Moving forward on career or educational goals

Routines & follow-through

  • Putting skills and strategies into daily practice
  • Building routines and practical systems that support change
  • Creating accountability for goals that have remained stuck

Therapy or coaching?

Some topics overlap. The purpose and kind of support are different.

Therapy

Clinical care to understand, process, and treat what is affecting your mental health.

  • Mental health symptoms and significant emotional distress
  • Clinical assessment and diagnosis where appropriate
  • Trauma processing and treatment planning
  • Psychiatric or psychological conditions
  • Deeper exploration of longstanding patterns
Explore therapy

Coaching

Non-clinical support to apply existing insight, plan, act, and follow through.

  • Concrete goals, decisions, and next steps
  • Boundaries, communication, and relationships
  • Behavioral change and life transitions
  • Practical systems and implementing strategies
  • Accountability and following through on specific goals
Explore coaching

Coaching is not psychotherapy and does not replace mental health treatment. It can complement therapeutic work for people who are ready to focus on specific goals, decisions, and next steps.

Is coaching a fit?

Coaching may be a fit if you have insight into what you want to change and are ready to focus on putting it into practice. That understanding might come from therapy or other meaningful personal work.

You might have a specific decision to make, a goal you keep putting off, or a skill you understand but find hard to use in daily life.

If you need support for mental health symptoms, significant emotional distress, or trauma processing, therapy may be the more appropriate service. Coaching does not substitute for needed clinical care.

What that can look like

You know where a boundary belongs.
Plan what to say, practice the conversation, and decide how to maintain it.
You have a career or educational goal.
Break it into manageable steps and create a plan for following through.
You understand a pattern you want to change.
Choose a specific daily behavior to practice and build a routine that supports it.

Start with a next step

If this sounds like the support you are looking for, get in touch about coaching. You do not need to share symptoms, trauma narratives, or other sensitive personal information in the inquiry form.

Ask about coaching

A clear scope of support

Coaching is a non-clinical service, separate from my psychotherapy practice. It does not establish a therapist-client relationship or include formal mental health assessment, diagnosis, psychotherapy, or treatment of trauma or other mental health conditions. It is not a substitute for mental health treatment or crisis services. If your needs fall outside the scope of coaching, we will discuss appropriate referrals.
