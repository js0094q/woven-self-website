Beyond the Letters: Peer Support After Greek Life

Separate men’s and women’s peer-support groups for people navigating difficult college and Greek-life experiences, including coercive control, pressure, and belonging.

The group is intended to offer connection, shared perspective, and discussion of boundaries and support. You would not need to tell your whole story to express interest.

Group details

  • Separate groups: A men’s group and a women’s group, each with its own participants.
  • Eligibility: Adults 18+ who have left college and had fraternity or sorority involvement, including pledging without joining. No diagnosis, formal report, or identification as a survivor is required.
  • Format: Six weekly, 75-minute sessions with the same six to eight participants in each group.
  • Cost: $25 per session ($150 for the six-session series). Ask about a reduced-fee place.
  • Tentative start: November 2026, subject to sufficient enrollment in each group. Exact dates and times will be confirmed before enrollment.

A brief fit conversation precedes acceptance. You may participate while receiving therapy elsewhere. An inquiry does not reserve a place or enroll you.

Peer support is not group therapy or crisis care. Group privacy expectations will be discussed before enrollment; confidentiality among participants cannot be guaranteed.
